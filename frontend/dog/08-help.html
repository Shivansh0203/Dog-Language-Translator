Frequently Asked Questions

Close
How do I create a PawAI account?

Select Get Started, enter your details, then add your dog's name, breed, age, and gender.

How do I add my dog profile?

Create your account with your dog's details, or use Account to update the profile later.

Can I use PawBot with voice input?

Yes. Choose the microphone in PawBot and allow browser microphone access when prompted.

What dog behavior can PawBot help explain?

PawBot can offer general guidance about barking, body language, appetite, energy, walks, and changes in routine.

Which browsers support voice recognition?

Recent versions of Chrome and Edge provide the most consistent speech-recognition support. Availability can vary by device and browser settings.

When should I contact a veterinarian?

Contact a veterinarian urgently for breathing trouble, collapse, severe pain, repeated vomiting, a serious injury, or sudden major behavior changes.
